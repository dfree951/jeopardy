# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Friends and family creating and playing trivia games together. A host manages the game while players watch a shared screen or join from their own devices. Large-screen readability matters.

## Product Purpose

Create reusable Jeopardy boards and play them with teams or individuals. Support host-controlled scoring and phone buzzers as separate game options. Keep answers off player screens until the host reveals them.

## Brand Personality

Familiar, direct, playful. Use a classic Jeopardy board and a simple editor. Labels should describe actions without slogans or promotional copy.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing headlines, decorative dashboards, and wordy onboarding. Use basic documentation and direct commit messages.

## Design Principles

- Keep editing separate from gameplay.
- Give the board and current clue the most space.
- Make host controls explicit and keep answers private until revealed.
- Preserve saved boards and active games across refreshes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast, keyboard controls, visible focus, reduced motion, and large clue text for a shared screen.
